# Code of conduct

LLM AdShield contributors should make participation safe, constructive, and welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific in technical discussion.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy. Never ask someone to publish captured media, credentials, transcripts, or identifying logs to prove a bug.
- Respect platform policies, copyright, DRM, and provider access controls.
- Accept a maintainer's decision to reject unsafe workarounds even when they would expand device compatibility.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, or publishing another person's private information is not acceptable. Neither is pressuring contributors to bypass device protections or misuse third-party services.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through the repository's private vulnerability-reporting channel when available. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation. They should handle reports confidentially, avoid conflicts of interest, and explain enforcement decisions to the affected parties when safe to do so.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
